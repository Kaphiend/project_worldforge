# SRD 5.2.1 content baseline

Worldforge uses the D&D System Reference Document 5.2.1 (2024 rules) as its
baseline for class skill lists, subclass identities, species, and supported
combat rules. The game keeps its own XP-purchase progression, attribute-point
advancement, resource model, and original summaries. Feats are not used.

The SRD's class features and subclass features are listed as trainer purchases.
Only mechanics with a matching runtime effect are playable; other feature
records are content drafts and do not imply that the effect is implemented.
Species and lineage trait identifiers are also descriptive unless a runtime
resolver handles them. Ability-score bonuses are not assigned by species.

Worldforge's Hide action follows SRD 5.2.1's DC 15 Dexterity (Stealth) check,
cover/line-of-sight gate, and Perception detection check. Arena obstacles
currently count as full cover. The exploration Sneak toggle is a compact
Worldforge mode: it fixes a Stealth result for the approach and halves movement
speed; mobs notice the character when their passive Perception meets or beats
that result. Species rules currently apply Keen Senses to passive Perception,
Wood Elf speed, Dwarven Toughness, poison and lineage damage resistance, and
Halfling Lucky to attack rolls. Fast Hands is available to a Thief who has
purchased the feature: press G during combat to make a bonus-action Sleight of
Hand check. Other Rogue features, active species traits, and environment
dependent traits such as Darkvision remain future work.
